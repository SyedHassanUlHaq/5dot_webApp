Getting started

How do I run my first scan?

From the Home tab, tap "Analyze now," choose a detection mode, then either choose a file from your device or paste a link (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or X). You'll be taken to a progress screen while it's analyzed, then to the result.

How long does a scan take?

Most scans finish within a minute or two, depending on the length of the clip and how busy our servers are. You can leave the screen and come back — the result will be waiting in your History tab once it's done.

File formats & limits

What file formats are supported?

Video: mp4, mov. Audio: mp3, m4a, wav, opus.

What's the maximum file size and length?

Up to 125 MB and 5 minutes per file. Direct uploads and pasted links are both subject to the same limit.

03

Detection modes

What's the difference between the three checks?

AI Voice Detection checks whether a voice is real or synthesized/cloned by AI. Deepfake Video Detection checks whether the footage itself was AI-generated or face-swapped. Lip-Sync Detection finds cut-and-splice edits, re-encoding jumps, and timeline discontinuities — regardless of whether AI was involved.

Can I run more than one check at once?

Yes — select as many detection modes as you like before submitting a scan; each one appears as its own result on the same report.

04

Accuracy & limitations

How accurate is 5dot?

Every result comes with a confidence score so you can see how certain the verdict is — tap "Show technical details" on any result to see it. 5dot's detection is automated and meant to help you make an informed judgment; it is a strong signal, not absolute proof.

Can 5dot detect all AI-generated content?

No tool can guarantee complete detection. New generation methods appear regularly, and very short clips have less signal to analyze. If a result looks wrong, use the "Wrong?" button on the result screen to let us know — it helps us improve.

Account & sign-in

How do I sign in?

With Google, with Sign in with Apple, or with an email and password — whichever you prefer. Switching providers later isn't supported, so sign in the same way each time.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete account. You'll be asked to confirm. All analyses, results, and personal data are deleted and cannot be recovered.

06

Plans & billing

What happens when I reach my monthly scan limit?

You'll see a notice before starting a new analysis once you hit your plan's monthly limit. Your existing results stay fully accessible, and the limit resets at the start of your next billing cycle.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 5dot → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → 5dot → Cancel. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. Refunds are handled through Apple's or Google's own refund process — contact us and we'll do what we can to help.
