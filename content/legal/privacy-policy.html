01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how 5dot ("5dot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service"), which let you upload or link audio and video for AI-generated content detection (AI voice, AI-generated video, and lip-sync/tamper analysis).

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy.

02

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), and optionally your first name, last name, and phone number. If you sign in with Google, Apple, Facebook, or Microsoft, we receive your name and email address from that provider instead.

Verification codes. When you sign up with email and password, we send a one-time verification code to your email to confirm you own the address before your account is activated.

Content you submit. The audio files, video files, and URLs you submit for analysis, along with the resulting verdicts, confidence scores, and evidence details we generate.

Usage and device data. Information about how you use the Service, such as scan counts, feature usage, app crash and performance diagnostics, your device's push-notification token (so we can deliver notifications to you), and IP address at the time of each request.

Billing information. If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is handled directly by Stripe, Apple, or Google, depending on how you subscribe. We receive confirmation of your subscription status and plan, but we do not receive or store your full card number.

Public content metadata. Our "Trending" feature displays publicly available metadata about popular videos (title, channel name, thumbnail, and view count) sourced from the YouTube Data API, together with aggregate, non-identifying scan statistics contributed by our user base for that public content.

03

How we use your information

We use the information described above to: operate and provide the Service, including running AI detection analysis on content you submit; create and secure your account, including email verification and password reset; process subscription payments and enforce plan limits; send you service-related communications, including verification codes, security alerts, and (if you opt in) push notifications; monitor, maintain, and improve the reliability and accuracy of the Service; and detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

04

How we share your information

We share information with service providers who help us operate the Service, each acting on our behalf and under contractual confidentiality obligations:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — for cloud storage of uploaded media and our application database.
  • Google, Apple, Facebook, and Microsoft — when you choose to sign in using one of these providers.
  • Stripe, Apple, and Google — to process subscription payments.
  • Our email delivery provider — to send verification codes and account-related emails.
  • Google (YouTube Data API) — to retrieve public video metadata for the Trending feature.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights, property, or safety of 5dot, our users, or the public, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy continuing to apply to your information.

05

Data retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Uploaded media and analysis results are retained so you can view your scan history, until you delete your account. Verification codes expire automatically after 5 minutes and are discarded whether or not they are used. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

06

Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit, hashed storage of passwords, and access controls on our cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your name, phone number, and avatar directly in the app's Profile screen. To request a copy of your data, request deletion of your account, or ask any other privacy question, contact us at thefivedot@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

08

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

09

International data transfers

We use cloud infrastructure that may process and store information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards to protect information transferred internationally.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before the changes take effect. Continued use of the Service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

11

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at thefivedot@gmail.com.
