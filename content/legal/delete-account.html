Delete your account

You can permanently delete your 5dot account and its data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us. This page explains both options and exactly what gets removed.

01

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. 1Open the 5dot app and sign in.
  2. 2Go to Profile → Privacy & Security.
  3. 3Tap Delete account, then confirm when prompted.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately upon confirmation — there's no waiting period or additional approval step.

02

Don't have the app, or can't sign in?

Email thefivedot@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request." Include your registered email address in the message. We'll verify the request and delete your account within a reasonable period, and confirm by email once it's done.

03

What gets deleted, and what we keep

Deleting your account removes your personal information and content from 5dot. A limited set of records may be retained where we're legally required to, as described below.

Deleted
May be retained
  • Email, name, phone number, avatar
  • Password hash
  • Uploaded audio and video files
  • Scan history, verdicts, and evidence details
  • Push-notification token
  • Linked Google sign-in association
  • Records required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes
  • Aggregate, de-identified statistics that can no longer be linked back to you
Note: deletion is permanent and can't be undone. There's currently no way to delete an individual scan without deleting your whole account — deleting your account is the only way to remove your data from 5dot.
04

Questions

For anything else about how your data is handled, see our Privacy Policy, or contact us at thefivedot@gmail.com.
